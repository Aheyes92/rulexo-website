Operational systems

Seven connected systems.
One operational infrastructure.

Each system is built around your existing workflows and integrates with the tools you already use. No replacement required. No new processes to learn. Operational enhancement, not operational disruption.

System 01

Project Intelligence Systems

What it does

Centralises your project knowledge — documents, contracts, decisions, site notes, correspondence — and makes it searchable. Ask a question in plain language and get the right answer immediately, sourced from your own project records.

What it solves

Stops operational knowledge being trapped in people's heads or buried in email threads. New site managers can get up to speed instantly. Disputes are resolved with accurate records. Nothing gets lost when a team member leaves.

  • Searchable project history across all live and completed contracts
  • Instant retrieval of previous decisions, scope agreements, and correspondence
  • Centralised operational knowledge accessible to the whole team

System 02

Site Reporting Automation

What it does

Converts voice notes, brief messages, or form inputs from site into structured daily reports, progress summaries, and stakeholder updates. Delivered automatically to the right people in the right format — without a site manager spending their evening writing them up.

What it solves

Eliminates the two-hour daily reporting task that eats into operational time. Stakeholders stay informed without constant chasing. Issues and delays get logged properly. Project progress is visible in real time rather than at the end of the week.

  • Voice note on site → structured daily report sent automatically
  • Issue and delay tracking with automatic flagging
  • Weekly progress summaries for client and principal contractor updates

System 03

Communication Coordination

What it does

Structures and automates the flow of project communications — update digests, team notifications, subcontractor briefings, and client-facing summaries. The right information reaches the right people without manual coordination or the risk of something being missed.

What it solves

Replaces fragmented WhatsApp threads, buried email chains, and the verbal handoffs that get forgotten. Reduces the time project managers spend coordinating communication and the delays caused when information doesn't reach the right person in time.

  • Daily project digests delivered to relevant team members automatically
  • Subcontractor briefings and site access instructions coordinated systematically
  • Escalation workflows that flag unresolved issues to the right level

System 04

Operational Dashboards

What it does

Gives directors and operations managers a live view of project status, workflow progress, compliance position, and action items — without needing to chase individual updates from site or project managers.

What it solves

Eliminates the information gap between the office and site. Decisions get made on accurate, current data rather than last week's update. Issues that would previously sit unnoticed get surfaced and escalated before they become delays.

  • Live project status across all active contracts
  • Delay tracking and milestone monitoring with automatic alerts
  • Compliance position and outstanding action items visible at a glance

System 05

Document & Knowledge Systems

What it does

Generates construction documentation from project details — RAMS, COSHH assessments, method statements, variation orders, toolbox talks — and organises existing documents into a searchable operational library accessible to your whole team.

What it solves

Removes the 90-minute documentation session before site can start. Ends the situation where a document exists but no one can find it. Gives new team members immediate access to company knowledge without relying on institutional memory.

  • RAMS, method statements, and COSHH generated in minutes from project information
  • Variation orders and site instructions produced from brief notes or site photos
  • Company document library made searchable — specs, contracts, previous RAMS

System 06

Compliance Operations

What it does

Tracks certifications, insurance, CSCS cards, plant operator licences, and safety documentation across your workforce and supply chain. Monitors expiry dates, triggers renewal prompts, and maintains an organised compliance record without manual spreadsheet management.

What it solves

Stops compliance failures from slipping through. Prevents site delays caused by expired documentation. Removes the manual burden of tracking across dozens of subcontractors. Keeps records audit-ready without a dedicated administrator.

  • Certification and insurance expiry monitoring with advance renewal alerts
  • Subcontractor compliance tracking across your supply chain
  • Safety documentation organised, filed, and retrievable on demand

System 07

Estimating & Tender Support

What it does

Handles the operational side of your tender pipeline — instant acknowledgement of new enquiries, project scope summaries, tender preparation support, and systematic follow-up. Keeps your tender pipeline moving without the process falling on one person.

What it solves

Stops tender windows being missed because a response was slow or a follow-up was forgotten. Removes the bottleneck when enquiries arrive during a busy site period. Ensures every principal contractor enquiry is acknowledged professionally and immediately — whatever time it arrives.

Workflow examples
  • Instant professional acknowledgement of every new enquiry — including out of hours
  • Automated follow-up on day 2 and day 5 if no response received
  • Project scope summaries prepared from enquiry details to support estimating
  • Tender status tracking across your active pipeline
  • Win/loss tracking to inform future tender strategy
